Design Principles

The Brutalist
Type Philosophy

01

Type Is Structure

Letterforms replace images, icons, and decorative elements as the primary building material of the layout. Typography carries every ounce of the visual and structural weight.

02

Scale Communicates Hierarchy

The size difference between elements is the primary navigation cue, not color or iconography. A 14rem headline beside 0.85rem body copy -- the gap is the point.

03

Rawness Is Intentional

Unpolished, system-default, or deliberately harsh typographic choices signal authenticity and rejection of corporate smoothness. If it looks too clean, reintroduce tension.

04

Friction Over Comfort

Layouts are meant to provoke attention, not provide frictionless scanning. Discomfort is a valid design outcome. The collision of massive and tiny generates visual dynamism.
