Design Principles

The Retro Anime Philosophy

Narrative interfaces, emotional color, hand-drawn character, expressive typography

Interface as Narrative

Treat the interface as a story — guide users through content like panels in a manga chapter. Asymmetric layouts, dramatic pacing, and visual hierarchy create a cinematic reading experience.

Color Is Emotion

Warm tones signal energy and action. Cool tones signal calm and atmosphere. Vivid accent colors pop against dark backgrounds with theatrical intensity, never soft or muted.

Embrace Imperfection

Slight texture, grain, and hand-drawn qualities add warmth that sterile digital precision lacks. The analog craft of paint-on-celluloid production has character that perfection cannot replicate.

Outlines Are Structural

Every major element should feel "inked" with deliberate thick borders. Removing outlines makes the design lose its illustrative, hand-drawn character — the cel-animation signature.

Visual Effects

Cel-Shaded Depth

Hard-edged shadows with zero blur and flat half-tone fills replicate hand-painted cel animation depth.
