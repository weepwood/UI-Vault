Chromatic Blur typography sits atop diffused color beds with careful contrast management, using semi-transparent background chips or subtle text shadows to remain legible against the shifting hues beneath. Generous letter-spacing and line-height create an airy, unhurried reading rhythm.

Component Cards

Color Field Backgrounds

Oversized radial gradients with heavy Gaussian blur create painterly backgrounds that shift and breathe beneath your content.

Layered Transparency

Multiple semi-transparent color layers stack to produce rich, complex color interactions. Let the eye blend rather than the computer.

Bokeh Decorations

Circular, semi-transparent shapes of varying sizes simulate out-of-focus light points, adding depth and whimsy to every composition.

Frosted Glass Surfaces

Cards, modals, and navigation use backdrop-filter blur to extend the defocused language into every interactive element.

Ambient Animation

Color fields drift, pulse, and shift at glacial speeds -- creating a living, breathing background. The user senses movement rather than sees it.

Chromatic Separation

Offset color shadows mimic lens chromatic aberration -- red-shifted and blue-shifted layers create that signature defocused glow on headlines.
