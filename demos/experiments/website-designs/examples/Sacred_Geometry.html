Sacred Patterns

The Architecture of Creation

From the Flower of Life to Metatron's Cube, these patterns form the geometric blueprint underlying all visible form — the mathematics of the cosmos made visible.

Flower of Life

Nineteen interlocking circles arranged in hexagonal symmetry, representing the interconnectedness of all living things and the geometric foundation of creation.

Pattern Unity

Golden Ratio

The divine proportion of 1.618, known as phi, governs the spiral of nautilus shells, the branching of trees, and the proportions of the human body.

Proportion Phi

Metatron's Cube

Thirteen circles connected by seventy-eight lines containing all five Platonic Solids — the most complex and symbolically rich figure in sacred geometry.

Complexity Platonic

Fibonacci Spiral

The logarithmic spiral derived from the Fibonacci sequence appears in nautilus shells, sunflower seed heads, spiral galaxies, and the unfurling of fern fronds.

Spiral Growth

Vesica Piscis

The almond-shaped intersection of two circles of equal radius, a symbol of duality unified — the womb of creation from which all other forms emerge.

Duality Creation

Platonic Solids

The five regular polyhedra — tetrahedron, cube, octahedron, dodecahedron, and icosahedron — the only shapes with identical faces, edges, and angles.

Form Perfection

Proportion, Symmetry & Stillness

The mathematics of the cosmos made visible through precise geometric construction

Foundation

Sacred Design Principles

Every element's size, position, and proportion derives from a governing geometric principle — design that feels simultaneously ancient and timeless, spiritual and rational.

Mathematical Intention

Every element's size and position references a geometric ratio derived from sacred proportions. No arbitrary placement — only phi-governed precision.

Symmetry as Order

Radial, bilateral, and rotational symmetry govern compositions. Asymmetry is used sparingly, deliberately, and always with mathematical justification.

Light from Darkness

The core visual metaphor is illumination — golden geometry shining against the cosmic void. Luminous gold on deep dark fields evokes celestial maps.

Reverence for the Void

Empty space is not wasted but the void from which form emerges. Generous negative space allows geometric patterns the room to breathe and be contemplated.

Visual Effects

CSS Techniques & Sacred Patterns

All geometry rendered through pure CSS — no image assets needed. Gradients, repeating patterns, and mathematical precision create the sacred vocabulary.

Flower of Life

Overlapping radial gradients create the iconic interlocking circle pattern using hexagonal background positioning.

Radial Emanation

Concentric circle rings radiating from a luminous center point, evoking divine light emerging from the void.

Hexagonal Grid

Angled linear gradients tessellate into nature's most efficient tiling shape — the honeycomb pattern.

Golden Spiral

Nested circular arcs and diagonal lines approximate the logarithmic spiral derived from the Fibonacci sequence.

Cosmic Starfield

Scattered radial dot gradients at varying sizes and opacities simulate a deep-space star field with gentle drift.

Sacred Grid Lines

Orthogonal and 60-degree repeating line patterns intersect to form a construction grid with concentric circle overlays.
