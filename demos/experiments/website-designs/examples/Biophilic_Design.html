Design Principles

The Biophilic Philosophy

organic flow, breathing space, sensory variability, restorative experience

Nature in the Space

Integrate direct references to natural elements through imagery, color, and living forms. Every surface should echo the textures, tones, and rhythms found in forests, rivers, and gardens.

Natural Analogues

Employ indirect references to nature through organic shapes, fractal patterns, biomorphic forms, and natural material textures that evoke the living world without literal depiction.

Restorative Experience

Every design decision reduces cognitive load and creates calm. Generous whitespace, gentle animation, and soft color harmonies work together to restore focus and well-being.

Sustainability as Aesthetic

Lightweight SVG graphics, efficient code, compressed assets, and dark mode options embody the environmental values the design communicates. Beauty and responsibility as one.
