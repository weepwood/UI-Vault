// Components //

Chaos Cards

No element should match its neighbor. Internal inconsistency is the goal. If two components look like they belong to the same system, one of them needs to change.

Break Rules With Knowledge

Understand conventional design deeply enough to violate it with surgical precision. Ignorant chaos is just a broken website.

principle
###

Tension As Composition

Use clashing colors, misaligned elements, and scale conflicts to create visual friction that demands attention and refuses to let the eye rest.

technique
***

Emotion Over Usability

Prioritize visceral impact, surprise, and memorability over frictionless user experience. Make them feel something first.

philosophy
!!!

Controlled Entropy

The chaos must feel intentional, not accidental. Every broken grid line and clashing color is a conscious decision placed with care.

core
@@@

Overwhelm As Engagement

The viewer should feel the visual equivalent of walking into a room where everything is happening at once. Overstimulation drives exploration.

strategy
&&&

Humor And Irreverence

Chaos Design should never take itself too seriously. Absurdity, playfulness, and visual jokes are essential ingredients of every composition.

attitude

// Effects Showcase //

CSS-Only Chaos

Distorted and glitchy visual effects signal digital rebellion. Warped visuals, chromatic aberration, scan-line artifacts, and animated unpredictability.

GLITCH

Glitch Distortion

Chromatic text splitting with clip-path animation. Cyan and magenta layers shift independently.

Chromatic Aberration

Split-channel color shift creates RGB separation. Simulates broken screen or analog distortion.

NEON

Neon Pulse

Glowing text-shadow oscillates between neon green and hot magenta. Digital sign flicker energy.

Noise Grain Texture

SVG fractal noise overlay with mix-blend-mode. Adds raw, unpolished surface texture to any element.

Collision Gradients

Overlapping radial gradients in clashing neon hues with animated hue-rotation and saturation shift.

Stripe Interference

Three layers of repeating diagonal stripes in clashing colors create animated moire-like visual static.

Smash These

Hover effects should surprise. If the user can guess what will happen, the chaos is too tame. Each button variant clashes with its neighbors deliberately.
