// Case.files

Design Elements

Dossiers from the investigation -- each card a piece of the puzzle.

01

Dithering Patterns

Checkerboard and ordered dithering create mid-tones and atmospheric fog without introducing new colors. The pattern is the texture -- embrace the grid.

Texture Atmosphere
02

Chiaroscuro Lighting

Harsh pools of light surrounded by deep shadow, mimicking the high-contrast cinematography of classic noir. Light is earned, never given freely.

Contrast Mood
03

Bitmap Typography

All text rendered on the pixel grid with no anti-aliasing. Monospaced and mechanical -- like typewritten case reports from a 1940s police station.

Pixel Font Monospace
04

Terminal Readouts

Data displays with amber or green phosphor glow, mimicking old CRT monitors. Status readouts, case numbers, and timestamps reinforce the investigative narrative.

CRT Data
05

Neon Sign Accents

Occasional bright color used sparingly to simulate glowing signage against dark cityscapes. One or two neon accents per viewport -- never more.

Glow Restraint
06

Single-Pixel Borders

UI elements use crisp 1px borders with no rounded corners, no gradients, and no soft shadows. Every line is hard, every edge deliberate.

Borders Minimal

// Render.effects

CSS Effects

Pure CSS techniques that bring the noir atmosphere to life -- no images required.

Checkerboard Dithering

Dual 45-degree gradients at 4px scale create authentic pixel dither patterns for mid-tone simulation.

Venetian Blind Shadows

Skewed repeating gradients cast horizontal stripe shadows -- the iconic motif of film noir cinematography.

Neon Flicker

Keyframe-driven text-shadow flicker simulates a faulty neon bar sign buzzing in a rain-soaked alley.

CRT Vignette

Radial gradient darkening at edges replicates the barrel distortion and phosphor fade of old CRT monitors.

Rain Streaks

SVG data-URI rain drops animated in a linear loop -- pixel-scale precipitation on a midnight backdrop.

Terminal Glow

Amber text with soft glow shadows on a void background, complete with a blinking cursor for authenticity.
