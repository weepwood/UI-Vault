Design Principles

The Rustic Modern Philosophy

material honesty, structural clarity, warmth through restraint

Material Honesty

Every texture, color, and surface should reference a real material without artificial embellishment or synthetic sheen. Wood looks like wood. Metal is brushed and raw, not chrome-polished to a mirror shine. Let surfaces be what they are.

Tension Between Raw & Refined

The aesthetic lives in the contrast between rough natural elements and clean modern geometry. Neither side should dominate. A weathered beam against a minimal white wall. An iron bracket holding a floating shelf. The interplay creates depth.

Structural Clarity

The underlying grid, hierarchy, and information architecture should be immediately legible, like the exposed beams of a well-built structure. Clear sightlines through the content. Visible container boundaries. Functional beauty in every element.

Patina Over Perfection

Embrace the visual character of age, wear, and use. Imperfection communicates authenticity and human touch. Design choices should feel timeless and durable, not trend-chasing -- aging as gracefully as the materials they reference.
