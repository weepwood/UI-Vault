Visual Effects

CSS Celestial Techniques

all the cosmic atmosphere, no image assets needed

Star Field

Scattered radial-gradient dots of varying size and opacity simulate a deep starfield across the night sky.

Nebula Gradient

Overlapping elliptical gradients in indigo, plum, and rose create atmospheric depth like a galactic cloud.

Iridescent Shimmer

A rotating multi-color gradient on a pseudo-element produces a shifting prismatic shimmer effect.

Gold Halo

Radial gradients with layered box-shadows create a pulsing celestial halo reminiscent of distant stars.

Constellation Map

Radial-gradient star points with thin connecting line gradients form constellation-like patterns.

Aurora Waves

Animated elliptical gradients in teal, lilac, and rose drift to simulate the northern lights.
