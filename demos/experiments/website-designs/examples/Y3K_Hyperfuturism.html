Design Principles

The Y3K Philosophy

Aspirational, alien, and otherworldly -- not dystopian, not gritty, not terrestrial.

01

Every Surface Is a Display

Nothing is inert. Every element can glow, shift, or respond. Treat the interface as a living, programmable membrane rather than static paper.

02

Color Is Light

Every colored element should appear to emit its own luminescence, not merely be painted on a surface. Neons glow against the void of space.

03

Depth Over Flatness

Use layering, parallax, and perspective transforms to suggest three-dimensional space. The interface should feel volumetric, not flat.

04

Biological Animation

Motion should feel organic -- smooth, breathing, pulsing -- not mechanical or robotic. Like living organisms, interfaces shift with gentle rhythm.
