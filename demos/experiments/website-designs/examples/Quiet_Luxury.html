Design Principles

The Philosophy of Less

Confidence through omission, warmth through material, hierarchy through space

01

Restraint as Primary Decision

Let what you choose not to include carry the design. Remove until it breathes. If a page section feels full, it has too much. The absence of decoration itself becomes the luxury.

02

Negative Space as Material

Treat white space as the most valuable material in the composition. It is the cashmere of the layout. Build hierarchy through spacing and weight rather than color contrast or size extremes.

03

Unhurried Interactions

Every interaction should feel unhurried. Speed communicates anxiety; slowness communicates confidence. Transitions use 400–800ms durations with gentle easing, moving at the pace of a deep breath.

04

Material Warmth Without Decoration

Use texture and material references to create warmth without resorting to ornamentation. Design as though every pixel will be printed on heavyweight cotton stock: if it would look cheap on paper, it looks cheap on screen.
