// 003 -- Components

Cards of Chaos

Each card is a collision of misaligned borders, aggressive type, and deliberate rotation. Nothing aligns. Nothing matches. That is the point.

01

Break Rules With Intention

Understand conventional design deeply enough to violate it with purpose. Chaos without knowledge is just mess. Every broken grid is a conscious decision.

02

Destroy the Grid

If a grid exists, violate it visually. Offset elements by inconsistent amounts. Let content bleed across column boundaries. Alignment is the enemy.

03

Embrace Digital Glitch

Warped images, pixel displacement, chromatic aberration, and scan-line artifacts signal digital rebellion. Corruption is the new composition.

04

Scale Extremes

Combine absurdly large elements with tiny ones in the same viewport. Conventional size hierarchy does not apply. Invert the expectations.

05

Rotation and Skew

Apply small random rotations and skew transforms to elements that should be straight. Nothing is level. Nothing is plumb. Stability is boring.

06

Layer Everything

Overlap text on images, images on other images, UI chrome on content. Z-index warfare creates unexpected stacking orders. Depth through collision.
