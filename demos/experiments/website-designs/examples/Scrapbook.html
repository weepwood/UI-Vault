Design Principles

The Scrapbook Philosophy

imperfection, warmth, layering, and personal touch

✦

Imperfection Is Intentional

Wobbly edges, uneven spacing, and casual placement are features, not flaws. They communicate that a real person assembled this page with their own hands and their own heart.

✎

Personal Over Polished

Every element should feel chosen and placed by a real person with their own taste and memories. Warmth and approachability matter more than pixel-perfect precision.

☰

Layering Creates Depth

Overlapping elements at various angles and z-indexes produce the tactile depth of a physical scrapbook. Shadows beneath papers give the illusion of real stacked materials.

🎨

Decoration Serves Connection

Washi tape, stickers, and embellishments create visual relationships between elements. A tape strip connects a photo to its caption. A sticker marks a moment as special.
