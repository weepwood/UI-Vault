Components

Soft, Grounded & Tactile

rounded edges, warm shadows, natural material inspiration

Curved Silhouettes

Rounded edges, soft arcs, and flowing forms replace sharp corners. Generous border-radius values of 12-24px evoke natural, organic shapes throughout the interface.

Shape Layout

Biophilic Integration

Botanical illustrations, organic SVG elements, and nature-inspired color palettes create a persistent, calming connection to the natural world.

Nature Pattern

Natural Textures

Wood grain, linen weave, stone surfaces, and ceramic finishes appear as subtle background textures, grounding the digital experience in physicality.

Texture Material

Grounded Shadows

Box shadows are soft, warm-toned, and suggest gentle weight. Elements rest on surfaces rather than floating dramatically, creating a calm, settled atmosphere.

Depth CSS

Slow, Deliberate Motion

Transitions are gentle and unhurried with 300-500ms ease-out curves. Subtle fades and smooth transforms replace bouncy or snappy effects for natural feel.

Animation UX

Generous Whitespace

Ample breathing room around every element creates a calm, uncluttered experience. Whitespace is treated as sunlight in a room — an active design element.
