Components

Curated Design Elements

Each component reflects the disciplined simplicity and warm polish of the Clean Girl aesthetic

Luminous Surfaces

Soft gradients and subtle gloss effects that mimic the glass-skin look. Interfaces feel fresh, hydrated, and dewy rather than flat or matte.

Glow Gradient

Tonal Layering

Depth is created through subtle value shifts within the same warm hue family. Monochromatic layering replaces high-contrast color combinations.

Neutral Depth

Generous White Space

Breathing room is essential. Content is carefully spaced with wide margins, giving each element room to exist quietly and confidently.

Layout Spacing

Satin Textures

Subtle background textures that suggest smooth fabric, cream, or soft paper. Nothing rough or industrial -- everything feels like cashmere.

Texture Soft

Thin Elegant Borders

When borders appear, they are 1px, low-opacity, and warm-toned. They define without confining, adding structure with the lightest possible touch.

Borders Minimal

Seamless Transitions

Hover states and page transitions feel smooth and effortless. Animations are gentle and restrained, matching the no-effort philosophy.

Motion Hover
