Foundation

Warm Neutral Canvas

Pure white is replaced by creams, parchment, and warm off-whites that feel like natural light filtered through linen curtains.

Background Warmth

Texture

Natural Overlays

Subtle linen weaves, paper grain, and soft noise at 2-5% opacity give surfaces tactile depth and a sense of handcrafted care.

Linen Grain

Typography

Editorial Expression

Confident serif headlines at generous sizes create focal points. Type itself becomes the primary visual element, replacing decorative imagery.

Serif Display

Space

Generous Negative Space

Whitespace is treated as an active design element. Sections breathe, elements never crowd, and the eye is guided by what is absent.

Layout Rhythm

Color

Restrained Accents

A single muted accent hue — sage, terracotta, or amber — deployed sparingly for interactive elements and focal points only.

Sage Muted

Motion

Slow, Purposeful Animation

Transitions use 300-600ms easing curves. Animations reveal content gently. Hover states are subtle shifts, never dramatic transformations.

Ease Subtle

CSS Textures & Surfaces

Material honesty through subtle, intentional techniques

Linen Texture

Warm Gradient

Sage Breath

Barely-There Shadows

Paper Grain
